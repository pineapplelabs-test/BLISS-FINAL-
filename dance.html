Classes Conducted

Ages 4+

Bollywood Dance

High-energy, expressive Bollywood routines combining classical, folk, and modern styles.

Ages 5+

Classical Dance

Foundations of classical Indian dance, covering mudras, rhythmic footwork, and postures of Bharatanatyam & Kathak.

Adults

Zumba Fitness

High-energy cardio routines set to active Latin beats for full-body conditioning.

Ages 7+

Guitar Classes

From first chords to fingerpicking — a solid foundation built through patient, one-on-one guidance.

Ages 6+

Keyboard Classes

Structured training covering basic scales, chord theory, and classical/modern repertoires.

Ages 5+

Carnatic Vocal

Classical Indian vocal music. Master swaras, raga structures, and devotional compositions while improving pitch, breath control, and memory.
